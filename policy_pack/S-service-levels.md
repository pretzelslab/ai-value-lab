# S. Service levels and uptime credits

Harbourline Analytics. Pack version 1.1.0. Fictitious.

---

**S-1.** Harbourline commits to 99.9 percent monthly uptime on the Growth and Enterprise
tiers. The Starter tier carries no uptime commitment and is not eligible for any credit
under this document.

**S-2.** Uptime is measured across a calendar month. Scheduled maintenance announced at
least 72 hours in advance is excluded from the calculation. Unannounced maintenance is
counted as downtime.

**S-3.** Where monthly uptime falls below the commitment, the customer is eligible for a
credit calculated as a percentage of that month's subscription fee:

| Measured uptime | Credit |
|---|---|
| 99.0 to 99.89 percent | 10 percent |
| 95.0 to 98.99 percent | 25 percent |
| Below 95.0 percent | 50 percent |

**S-4.** A credit must be requested by the customer within 30 days of the end of the
affected month. Harbourline does not apply uptime credits automatically, and a request
made after 30 days is not eligible.

**S-5.** Uptime credits are issued as account credit and are governed by R-3. They are
never paid in cash and are never refunded to a payment method.

**S-6.** The total credit issued under S-3 in any single month may not exceed 50 percent
of that month's subscription fee, regardless of the number or duration of incidents.

**S-7.** Degraded performance is not an outage. A query running slowly, a dashboard taking
longer than usual to load, or a scheduled report arriving late do not count toward the
uptime calculation unless the service was unavailable.

**S-8.** Outages caused by the customer's own network, identity provider, data sources or
misconfiguration are excluded from the uptime calculation.

**S-9.** Harbourline publishes a status page and posts incident updates there. The status
page is the record of record for whether an incident occurred and how long it lasted.

**S-10.** Where a customer is on an annual plan, the monthly subscription fee for the
purpose of S-3 is one twelfth of the annual fee.
