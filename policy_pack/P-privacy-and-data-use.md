# P. Privacy and data use

Harbourline Analytics. Pack version 1.1.0. Fictitious.

This document covers how Harbourline uses customer data. Retention and deletion timetables
are in document D.

---

**P-1.** Harbourline does not use customer analytics data to train models. This applies to
data uploaded by the customer, data derived from it, and the content of dashboards and
queries. There is no opt in and no opt out, because the practice does not occur.

*(Previously numbered D-8. Moved to this document in pack version 1.1.0.)*

**P-2.** Harbourline collects aggregate product telemetry describing which features are
used and how often. Telemetry is collected at the account level, contains no customer
analytics data, and is not linked to individual end users.

**P-3.** For customer analytics data, the customer is the controller and Harbourline is the
processor. Harbourline processes that data only to provide the service and on the
customer's documented instructions.

**P-4.** A data processing agreement is executed separately from this pack and governs
Harbourline's processor obligations, sub-processor list, international transfer mechanism
and breach notification terms. Those terms are not reproduced here and support cannot
state them from this pack.

**P-5.** Harbourline staff access to customer data is limited to named support and
engineering roles, requires a documented reason, and is recorded in the audit log described
in D-5. Customers on Enterprise may request an access report covering the previous 13
months.
