# B. Billing and proration

Harbourline Analytics. Pack version 1.1.0. Fictitious.

---

**B-1.** Annual plans are invoiced in advance for the full term. Monthly plans are charged
on the same calendar day each month. Where that day does not exist in a given month, the
charge falls on the last day of that month.

**B-2.** An upgrade taken part way through a term is charged pro-rata from the date of the
upgrade to the end of the current term. The upgrade takes effect immediately.

**B-3.** A downgrade taken part way through a term takes effect at renewal, not immediately.
The customer keeps the higher tier until then. The value difference is handled under R-2.

**B-4.** Account credit is applied automatically against the next invoice before any charge
is made to a payment method. A customer holding credit larger than the invoice pays nothing
and carries the remainder forward, subject to the expiry in R-3.

**B-5.** Where a payment fails, Harbourline retries three times over 10 days and notifies the
billing contact on each attempt. If payment has not succeeded, the account is suspended on
day 14.

**B-6.** A suspended account retains its data under D-1 and can be restored by settling the
outstanding balance. Suspension does not start the deletion timetable in D-2.

**B-7.** An invoice must be disputed within 60 days of its issue date. After 60 days the
invoice is treated as accepted and is not adjusted.

**B-8.** Prices are exclusive of tax. Applicable taxes are calculated at the billing address
provided by the customer and added to the invoice.

**B-9.** A change of billing contact or billing address takes effect on the next invoice and
does not alter invoices already issued.

**B-10.** Harbourline may change list prices with 60 days notice. A price change does not
take effect during a term already paid for, and applies from the next renewal.

**B-11.** Payment terms for invoiced Enterprise customers are net 30 from the invoice date
unless a different term is recorded in a negotiated agreement.
