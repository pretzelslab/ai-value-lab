# R. Refunds and credits

Harbourline Analytics. Pack version 1.1.0. Fictitious.

---

**R-1.** A customer on an annual plan may request a full refund within 14 calendar days
of the initial purchase date. The 14 day window applies to the first purchase only and
does not reset on renewal. Monthly plans are not refundable at any point.

**R-2.** A reduction in plan tier or seat count taken part way through a term does not
produce a refund. The difference in value is issued as account credit under R-3.

**R-3.** Account credit is applied to the customer's Harbourline account. It is valid for
12 months from the date of issue, applies automatically against future invoices, is not
transferable between accounts, and has no cash value. Credit remaining after 12 months
expires and is not reinstated.

**R-4.** Where a refund is approved, it is issued to the original payment method. Funds
leave Harbourline within 10 business days of approval. Time taken by the customer's bank
or card issuer to post the funds is outside Harbourline's control.

**R-5.** An annual plan cancelled after the R-1 window remains active until the end of the
paid term. Service continues until that date. No pro-rata refund is made for the unused
portion.

**R-6.** A duplicate or erroneous charge caused by Harbourline is refunded in full within
5 business days of the customer reporting it. R-1 does not apply to duplicate charges.

**R-7.** Refunds are not available on the basis of low product usage. A customer who did
not use the product during a paid term is not entitled to a refund for that term.

**R-8.** Where a customer is entitled to both a refund under R-1 and a credit under R-2
in the same billing period, the refund is processed first and the credit is calculated on
the remaining balance.

**R-9.** Harbourline may issue a discretionary credit outside these terms. A discretionary
credit is a goodwill gesture, does not set a precedent, and must be approved by a manager.
Support staff cannot commit to a discretionary credit without that approval.
