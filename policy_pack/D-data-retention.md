# D. Data retention and deletion

Harbourline Analytics. Pack version 1.1.0. Fictitious.

---

**D-1.** Customer data is retained for the life of the subscription. A suspended account
retains its data on the same basis as an active one. Suspension is not cancellation.

**D-2.** On cancellation, customer data is retained for 30 days and is then permanently
deleted. The 30 day window exists so that a customer who cancels in error can be restored.
After it closes, restoration is not possible.

**D-3.** A customer may request a full export of their data at any time during the
subscription or during the 30 day window in D-2. The export is delivered within 5 business
days.

**D-4.** Where a customer requests deletion, deletion from live systems completes within
30 days. Copies held in backup are purged within a further 60 days, so the total elapsed
time before all copies are gone is up to 90 days.

**D-5.** Security audit logs recording authentication events and administrative actions are
retained for 13 months. They are retained regardless of a deletion request, because they are
required for security investigation. Audit logs do not contain customer analytics data.

**D-6.** Deletion is irreversible. Harbourline holds no mechanism to restore data once it
has been deleted under D-2 or D-4, and cannot recover it on request afterwards at any price.

**D-7.** Data residency is fixed at account creation. Customers in the EU have their data
stored in the EU. Residency cannot be changed in place. Moving an existing account to a
different region requires a migration, which is a scoped project rather than a setting.

**D-8.** *Superseded in pack version 1.1.0. This clause covered model training and product
telemetry, which are data use rather than data retention. Moved to P-1 and P-2. The number
is retained and not reused.*

**D-9.** Where a deletion request and an active dispute over an invoice exist at the same
time, deletion proceeds on the D-4 timetable. Billing records are not customer data and are
retained for the period required by law.
